Stockroom Privacy Policy

Effective August 20, 2026.

Local data

Your component libraries, drafts, applied designs, settings, and provider download staging are stored on your PC. Removing a library or uninstalling Stockroom removes data through the corresponding Windows and Stockroom controls.

Provider data

Stockroom sends part identifiers to configured Mouser and DigiKey APIs and opens only the third-party provider pages you choose. Those services process requests under their own privacy policies.

Credentials

Provider API secrets are stored in Windows Credential Manager when supported. Stockroom does not operate a credential service.

Telemetry

Stockroom has no Stockroom-operated advertising, analytics, or telemetry service.

Support

Questions and deletion help: Stockroom support.
