Tabler Outline 3.46.0 SVGs from @tabler/icons 3.46.0. They are fitted into
Stockroom's shared 24-pixel outline frame and inherit the application's current
colour. Copyright Pawel Kuna and contributors. License: MIT. Source:
https://github.com/tabler/tabler-icons. The MIT terms printed below apply.
